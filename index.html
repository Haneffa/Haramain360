STEP 01 · IHRAM

Prepare for Ihram & enter the state correctly

Use the 3D simulation above to practise the preparation sequence, then review the real photograph and guided video. Ihram is a sacred state entered with intention and Talbiyah at the applicable Miqat; the clothing is part of the preparation, but Ihram itself is the ritual state.

📍
Where is the niat made?At the designated miqat for the travel route. Air travellers should be prepared to enter ihram before crossing the miqat. If starting a new Umrah while already in Makkah, the applicable boundary guidance should be followed.

STEP 02 · TAWAF

Practise Tawaf in the VR environment

Enter the VR scene above, identify the Hajar al-Aswad start point and use the round controls to practise the seven-round sequence.

STARTHajar al-AswadAlign with the Black Stone and begin the first round.
→ 7 ROUNDS →
ENDHajar al-AswadReturning to the same alignment completes one round; the seventh ends at the starting point.
  • 01 Keep the Kaaba on your left and move counter-clockwise.
  • 02 Seven complete rounds constitute the Tawaf.

What is essential?

There is no special object required for Sa'i (Walking between Safa and Marwah) itself. Bring only permitted personal items according to current Haram guidance; keep the route clear and follow crowd-control instructions.

🕋 IHRAM ESSENTIALS

Know what to do — and what to avoid.

Use this quick guide before entering Ihram. The content is designed as a learning aid; pilgrims should confirm their circumstances with a qualified scholar or authorised pilgrimage guide.

QUIZ SCORE 0/8 Start the challenge
✓
DO

During Ihram

  • Enter Ihram at the appropriate Miqat before crossing it while intending Umrah or Hajj.
  • Make the intention and recite the Talbiyah.
  • Keep clothing and personal care consistent with the rules of Ihram.
  • Use unscented personal-care products where needed after entering Ihram.
  • Maintain patience, good manners, remembrance and avoid harming others.
!
DON'T

Things to Avoid

  • Do not apply perfume or fragranced products after entering Ihram.
  • Do not cut or remove hair or trim nails while in Ihram.
  • Do not engage in sexual relations or prohibited sexual conduct.
  • Men should not wear prohibited tailored garments or cover the head.
  • Do not hunt land game or commit other acts prohibited while in Ihram.
MEN

Male Ihram

Two unstitched sheets are normally used: izar and rida'. Avoid covering the head while in Ihram.

IzarRida'No head covering
WOMEN

Female Ihram

Women may wear normal modest clothing. Ihram does not require a special colour or a particular two-piece garment.

Modest clothingNo special colourFollow face-covering rules

Simple rule

Hajj-specific planning

Hajj packing depends on the official programme, accommodation and transport arrangements. Confirm any Mina, Arafat and Muzdalifah requirements with your licensed Hajj operator before adding specialised items.

Important: This is a practical packing planner, not a list of religious obligations. The Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah currently recommends essentials such as daily medication, health supplies, comfortable walking shoes, anti-slip socks, hand sanitiser, masks, sunscreen, an umbrella and a phone charger. It also advises that personal-care and medical products used during Ihram be fragrance-free and suitable for Ihram. Official Saudi Umrah Guide
